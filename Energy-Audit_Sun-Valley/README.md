# ISO Standard-Based Energy Audit & 10-Year Demand Forecasting for Barangay Sun Valley

## Problem Statement
We answered: "Which energy interventions yield the fastest cost recovery for Barangay Sun Valley Hall, and what does the 10-year demand trajectory require us to plan for now?"

## Audience
This project is for barangay officials and local government unit (LGU) energy managers making capital allocation decisions on energy efficiency upgrades.

## KPI or Key Metric
The main metric we track is monthly energy savings (kWh and Php) per intervention, with simple payback period (months) as the prioritization signal and Prophet-forecasted peak demand (kWh/month) as the forward planning anchor.

## Data Source
We explored Meralco electric bills from Barangay Sun Valley Hall, Parañaque City (November-December 2022-2023 and October-November 2024), collected directly via softcopies of billing statements during on-site audit. Also conducted a site inspection covering lighting systems, cooling equipment, and auxiliary systems while identifying potential energy inefficiencies.

## Result and Impact
Delivered a ranked intervention plan and a 10-year demand forecast using Prophet on the available Meralco billing data. Full implementation of LED retrofitting, unplugging idle electric fans, and eliminating phantom loads from TVs, rice cookers, and PCs cuts monthly consumption by 568.024 kWh (about 10%) and saves Php 78,387.45/year against a 5.73%-error-validated audit baseline, for a capital investment of Php 22,200 and a 4-month payback period. The forecast projects peak demand growing from 5,531.946 kWh/month in 2025 to 9,641.515 kWh/month by 2035 (74% increase), quantifying the capacity risk the barangay must plan for within the decade.
